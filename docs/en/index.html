I.T.E.M. Kulturális és Oktatási Alapítvány

A cultural and educational umbrella organisation in Budapest, since 2020

A link between education and research on the one hand and grassroots cultural and educational initiatives on the other: a book series, journals, a music academy, a residency and youth programmes.

  • Book series 2020–

    Kijárat book series

    Launched with Kijárat Kiadó in 2020, the series describes our digitalising everyday life in books that are scholarly yet accessible.

All projects

  1. 01 HANGZAVART? Music academy 2021– A non-academic music academy: a European network since 2021 whose summer residencies bring musicians of every background together to question the categories of music education.
  2. 02 Kijárat book series Book series 2020– Launched with Kijárat Kiadó in 2020, the series describes our digitalising everyday life in books that are scholarly yet accessible.
  3. 03 HOLY WEEK Residency and tour 2025 A week-long residency of eight international creators and a participatory performance tour through four countries on Central European Easter traditions.
    HOLY WEEK2025
  4. 04 Fresh Fabrik Erasmus+ partnership 2022–2024 An Erasmus+ partnership of cultural organisations from Malta, Estonia and Hungary, reacting to new skills needs in the cultural and creative sectors.
  5. 05 neurodiverz.xyz Youth project 2022–2023 A project initiated by young adults: a team of 25 neurodivergent people built an information website for peers diagnosed late or never.
  6. 06 HIBIKI Journal 2020–2023 A Japanese studies journal presenting Japanese culture in Hungarian, reliably and accessibly.
  7. 07 Introducing the Computational Stylistics Group Journal 2021–2022 A special issue of the journal Digitális Bölcsészet and a stylometry workshop on the work of the Kraków-based Computational Stylistics Group.
  8. 08 Mészöly 100 Student competition 2021 “From another camera angle…” – a silent-film project call for secondary-school students on the centenary of Miklós Mészöly’s birth.
