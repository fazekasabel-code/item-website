In 2020 we launched a joint book series with Kijárat Kiadó.

The volumes of the series describe our increasingly digital everyday lives and cultural practices in a way that is scholarly yet accessible. Alongside young Hungarian authors, we publish translations in order to offer as many perspectives as possible on understanding our contemporary world.
